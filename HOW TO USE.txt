=====================================================================
  CLAUDE VIDEO STUDIO - HOW TO USE  (no technical knowledge needed)
=====================================================================

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. START THE STUDIO
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Double-click:   Start Studio.bat

  - A black window opens and the studio opens in your web browser.
    (If the browser doesn't open, type this in the address bar:
     http://127.0.0.1:8070 )

  - KEEP THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN while you work. It IS the studio.
    You can minimise it. Closing it stops the studio.

  FIRST TIME ON A NEW COMPUTER:
  - The black window asks "Run Setup now? [Y,N]". Press  Y .
  - Setup downloads everything it needs (about 2.5 GB, 10-15 minutes).
    This happens only once. Next time the studio opens right away.
  - You can also run setup yourself: double-click  Setup.bat

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. EDIT A VIDEO (4 tabs at the top of the page)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  "+ New project"  -> give it a name.

  TAB 1  FOOTAGE
     Click "+ Add videos" (or drag files into the box). Several at once
     is fine; the same clip twice is refused.
     ORDER = the order of your video. Change it by:
        - dragging a clip card by its handle, or the arrow buttons
        - "Order: Sort by name / recording time / length"
        - the TIMELINE below: drag a clip block left/right
     Timeline: click a clip to select it (Ctrl/Shift for several),
     Play plays the selected clips one after another (Space = play).
     "Skip" leaves a clip out of the video without deleting it.
     Click "Analyze footage" (about 1 minute per minute of video).
     When it's done, a preview of the complete video renders by itself,
     and the green "Next: Scenes ->" button takes you to tab 4.
     Clips with no speech (music, street footage) get captions OFF
     automatically. To bring them back: "captions on" in that scene.

  TAB 2  REFERENCES & BRIEF  (your LOOK LIBRARY)
     Drag in sample videos you like. Each one gets a number (R1, R2...)
     and its own LOOK (all the style settings) and its own brief.
     "Edit look": click None / R1 / R2... - the settings and the
     "Brief for R1" box below show THAT look. Editing a look does NOT
     change your video. "Ask Claude to study" = Claude analyses the
     reference and fills in its look.
     "About this video" = what the video is about (shared by all looks).
     Every setting has a "?" - click it to see what it does and why.
     "Background audio" = None, or R1 / R2...'s background music under
     your voice: the studio takes that reference's VOICE out (Demucs
     voice-remover, about a minute the first time, then cached) and
     the music gets softer whenever you talk. It replaces "Music"
     while on. Music in someone else's video is usually copyrighted -
     fine to practise with, risky to publish.
     "Green screen" = what shows where your green cloth was (full screen
     AND split layout): Keep as filmed · Clean wall (colour) · Picture
     behind me · Video behind me. For picture / video pick a file in
     "Behind me" (from the Media tab) or click ⬆ to upload one. A video
     loops and runs on across cuts; its sound is never used.
     Click "Save brief & settings" when done.
     For Hindi videos set "Language" to Hindi before analyzing.

  TAB 3  MEDIA  (your own files - shared by every project)
     Drop audio, music, videos, transitions, stickers or pictures (many
     at once). The studio sorts each one into the right folder:
        sound up to 15 s  -> Sound effects   (Library/Sounds/My sounds)
        longer sound      -> Music           (Brand Kit/music)
        transparent PNG / WebP, any GIF -> Stickers (Library/Stickers/My stickers)
        photo             -> Pictures        (Library/Pictures)
        clip named "transition / swipe / wipe / light leak / glitch..."
        or up to 2.5 s    -> Transitions     (Library/Transitions)
        other clip        -> Videos          (Library/Videos)
     "Sort as" forces one kind for the next drop. The list under the drop
     area says where each file went (or why it was refused: not a media
     file, damaged, already there). Each card: click to play / listen,
     "move..." = wrong kind, 🗑 = delete (kept in Library/.trash).
     Music, sounds and stickers work at once in every project (Library
     panel, Music setting, "scene 5 money sticker"). Videos, transitions
     and pictures: click "+ this project" - then pick them in "Cover with
     B-roll" or on a timeline track.

  TAB 4  SCENES  (the main editor)
     THE STEPS (top bar, left to right):
       ① Plan story (Claude) – OPTIONAL, for raw / long footage: Claude
          plans keep / cut / reorder, the hook, cards, B-roll as a
          storyboard; you approve before anything changes. Short clips
          already in order? Skip it.
       ② ✨ Auto-edit – decorates the final cut (below). Run it AFTER ①:
          the plan may cut or reorder scenes. It warns you if a plan is
          still on its way / waiting for approval, and its report says
          "run again" if the story changed afterwards.
       💬 Tell the editor – the box under the top bar, for every change
          after that (below).
     ✨ AUTO-EDIT = the whole decoration in one click: pick the Look
     first, then click it.
     It reads every scene (what is said and WHEN) and adds:
        - a sticker that pops in on the word that matters (📈 profit,
          📉 loss, 💰 money, ⚠ risk, 💡 tip, 🤔 question, 👇 follow…)
          and vanishes, with a matching sound landing on that word
        - zoom moments on key words / numbers
        - transitions only where the topic changes (with a whoosh)
        - a slow zoom on scenes without speech, a fade-out ending
     Strength: Subtle / Balanced / Energetic (or "auto strength" =
     from your look's brief). The edited view then renders by itself.
     The report under the timeline lists what it did in each scene –
     click a scene to review / change it. Your own edits are never
     touched; running it again replaces only its own work; Undo or
     "remove the auto-edit" takes it all out. (It never uses the
     meme sounds – only the free licensed ones.)
     💬 TELL THE EDITOR (one box for every change, plain words):
        name a scene = that scene; no scene = the whole video.
          scene 6: calm music.  scene 5: money sticker and cash sound
          scene 3: zoom on 'double', no transition
          last scene thumbs up sticker top left · scenes 2-4 black and white
          remove silences · format 9:16 · captions upper   (whole video)
        Scenes: "scene 5", "scene five", "5th scene", "scenes 2-4",
        "scene 5 and 6", "last scene", "all scenes". Timing: "at 2 s",
        "on 'double'", "when I say double" (else the scene's key word).
        A sticker / sound you ask for replaces auto-edit's on that scene.
        Music needs files: put MP3s in Brand Kit/music (or Library/Music)
        named by mood – calm_…, upbeat_…, dramatic_… – it plays under
        that scene only, soft under your voice.
        Meme sounds only when you write "meme" (no licence – warned).
        Enter sends, Shift+Enter = new line. The list under the box shows
        ✓ done · ✗ refused (why) · → sent to Claude. One Undo per note.
     "Look" dropdown at the top = which look this video USES
     (None, ★ Brand Kit, R1, R2...). A NEW video starts on
     "None · simple settings". Pick a look and its settings apply
     right away; the edited view updates by itself. Your scene edits
     (cuts, trims, cards, caption height...) stay. Undo switches back.
     "▶ Your edited video" (right of the timeline) plays your
     video with every edit so far - it updates itself after each change;
     "Original footage" plays the raw camera clip.

     TIMELINE (under the player): one block per scene, as long as it
     is in the edited video. Click = select. Drag a block = move it.
     Drag a block's EDGE = trim it. Cut scenes show as small striped
     blocks. The "B-roll" lane shows cutaways, "Cards" the top cards.
     Keys: Space play · arrows next/previous scene · S split ·
     Delete cut/restore · I / O trim start/end to the playhead ·
     Ctrl+Z undo.
     COPY A SCENE: select it, "⧉ Duplicate" (or Ctrl+D) = a copy right
     after it. Or Ctrl+C, click another scene, Ctrl+V = the copy goes
     AFTER that scene. The copy keeps every edit (trim, speed, zoom,
     captions, card, text...), is a scene of its own (7 -> 8 scenes)
     and can be dragged anywhere. Ctrl+Z removes it.
     Command: duplicate   /   paste scene 3 (copy of scene 3 after
     the selected scene)

     INSPECTOR (right): buttons for the selected scene - cut, role,
     trim, speed, zoom, mute/volume, captions, caption POSITION
     (Auto / Head / Center / Below), pauses, B-roll, card, text.
     Click a WORD to fix how the captions write it.
     HOVER over any button or label to see what it does.
     Command: captions at head / center / below / auto
     EXACT HEIGHT: type a number in the % box next to Position
     (% from the TOP of the screen: 0 = top edge, 100 = bottom edge)
     and press Enter: EVERY scene gets that height (also scenes you add
     later). "only this scene" = an exception for the selected scene;
     its "follow the video" link undoes the exception. The small
     phone picture shows where it lands: dark blue = top card, red =
     covered by Instagram/YouTube's caption + buttons (keep it 15-78).
     ZOOM MOMENTS: a quick punch-in on a few words, INSIDE the scene -
     the scene stays one scene. Type  punch 1.2-2.5  (seconds in this
     scene; add a strength: punch 1.2-2.5 1.2). Shown in the Inspector
     under Zoom (click x to remove). Claude's storyboard punch-ins are
     zoom moments too - they never cut your scene into pieces.
     Text on screen never covers the captions - if both want the same
     place, the text moves above/below them. Long text wraps by itself.
     Music-only scenes: the speech recognizer "hears" fake words in
     music. They are hidden ("No speech detected") and captions stay
     off - unless you set Captions to On for that scene.

     EXTRA TRACKS ("＋ Track" above the timeline) - as many as you like:
        🎬 Video   an extra clip ON TOP, with its own sound (volume)
        🎞 B-roll  an extra clip/picture on top, silent
        🎵 Audio   music, voice-over or sounds (volume, fade in/out)
        🔤 Text    titles: title (middle), lower (bar above the
                   captions) or top (banner)
        Double-click an empty spot on a track = add something there
        (or drag a clip chip onto a Video/B-roll track). Drag a block =
        move it, drag its edge = make it shorter/longer, × = remove.
        Click a block: its settings open under the timeline (which
        file, length, where on screen, whole picture, volume, fades,
        ⬆ upload). Track name: double-click to rename. 👁/🔊 = hide or
        mute the track, × = delete it (Undo brings it back).
        Drag a SCENE from the Video lane UP onto a Video / B-roll /
        Audio track = it leaves the main line (the video closes the
        gap; the scene stays as a striped "cut" block) and its clip
        plays on that track where you drop it (Video: picture + sound,
        B-roll: picture only, Audio: sound only). Restore or Undo
        brings it back. Its captions / jump cuts stay with the scene.
        BACK DOWN: drag a Video / B-roll block onto Video 1 (between
        two scenes) = it becomes a scene again – a scene you lifted
        comes back WITH its captions, cuts and settings (trimmed the
        way the block was). Only footage clips can be scenes; uploads
        and pictures stay on tracks. Drag a block onto ANOTHER track
        to move it there (Video <-> B-roll, Audio, Text: same type).
        Video tracks have 🔊 (sound) and 👁 (picture) switches.
        EMPTY ZONE under the tracks: drop a clip chip or drag a scene
        there and a new track is made for it (double-click it = the
        + Track menu).
        TRACK HEADERS (left of every lane): 🔓/🔒 lock (no accidental
        changes) · 👁 hide (B-roll, Cards, picture tracks) · 🔊 mute
        (Video 1 = your scenes' own sound, Audio tracks).
        PLAYHEAD (red line): drag its top knob to scrub; ✂ on it
        splits the scene there (key S – works in both player views).
        ZOOM: − / Fit / + above the timeline, or Ctrl + mouse wheel.
        Scene blocks show their frames and, in green, their sound.
        Each block is CONNECTED to the scene where it starts: move,
        trim or speed up that scene and the block moves with it.
        Higher tracks are drawn on top; captions always stay on top.
     EVERY scene of every clip is a block on the Video lane - also clips
     with nobody speaking (market footage etc.). Drag any block left or
     right to put it before/after another scene. That's all you need.

     B-ROLL FOR ONE SCENE (Inspector, "Cover with B-roll"):
        - "⬆ Upload video / picture" = pick any video or picture
          (chart, screenshot...) from your computer: it covers THIS
          scene right away. It is kept with the project and listed
          under "Uploaded B-roll" for other scenes (no second copy).
        - Placement: Full screen / Top / Bottom.
            split layout:  Top = the card panel, Bottom = over YOU
                           (the card stays on top)
            full layout:   Top half / Bottom half (you in the other
                           half, an orange line between)
          The small phone picture shows the covered part in orange.
        - Picture: "Fill (crop)" fills the space (edges cut off if the
          shape differs) or "Whole picture" (nothing cut off; the empty
          space is a soft blurred copy of it - best for charts and
          screenshots). Command: broll fit / broll fill
        - Captions on it: On / Off for this scene.
        - "Manage uploads": see every uploaded file, which scenes use
          it, and 🗑 delete. Scenes using it show you again. Undo
          brings the file and the scenes back.
        - Videos: "Starts in the clip at" (seconds). Pictures stay
          on screen for the whole scene. Your voice always keeps
          playing; B-roll never makes the video longer.
     ✨ LIBRARY (Inspector, under B-roll): four tabs for the selected
     scene. Everything is free to use (licences: Library\LICENSES.md).
        ⇄ Transition  how the video goes INTO this scene: 47 kinds
                      (fade, slide, wipe, circle, zoom, pixelate...).
                      Hover a tile to preview it, click to use it.
                      Pick the time (0.25-1 s) and the whoosh sound.
                      "use at every cut" = the whole video.
                      The video never gets longer: the next voice
                      starts exactly at the cut.
        ✨ Effects     flash, fade in/out, punch-in zoom, slow zoom,
                      camera shake, glitch, black & white, vintage,
                      cinematic... (captions + stickers stay steady).
        🙂 Stickers    1,600 3D emoji. "Finance picks" first (rocket,
                      money bag, chart, fire, warning...), or search.
                      Click to add; then set when (at), how long
                      (for), where, size and pop/bounce/still.
        🔊 Sounds      320 sound effects (whoosh, pop, cash register,
                      ding, riser, typing...). ▶ listens. "+ add" puts
                      it in the scene; "hits at" = the second where
                      its hit lands (it starts a bit earlier so the
                      hit is exactly there).
     Your own: put PNG/GIF stickers in Library\Stickers\My stickers and
     WAV/MP3 sounds in Library\Sounds\My sounds, then click ↻.
     First time on a PC: the Library downloads in Setup (or click
     "Download the free Library" in that panel, ~120 MB).
     Commands: transition slide left 0.5 · transition off ·
        transitions fade (whole video) · effect shake · effect remove
        shake · sticker rocket at 1 for 2 top-right size 30% ·
        sticker 1 at 0.5 · sticker remove 1 · sfx whoosh-fast-hard at
        0.2 · sfx 1 vol 50% · sfx remove 1
     B-ROLL (optional): a clip can instead COVER a speaker scene while
     your voice keeps playing (top panel in the split layout, full
     screen otherwise). B-roll scenes show as small blue 🎞 blocks;
     drag one back to a place in the video to play it there again.
     ON THE TIMELINE: all your clips are shown as chips above it.
        - drag a clip chip ONTO the middle of a scene -> it covers the scene
          (your voice keeps playing)
        - drag a B-roll clip chip onto the EDGE of a scene (orange line)
          -> it goes INTO the video as its own scene there, with its own
          sound - e.g. an intro before scene 2
        - drag a video block UP into the B-roll lane -> it becomes B-roll
        - drag a blue B-roll block left/right -> move it to other scenes
        - drag its left/right EDGE -> cover more or fewer scenes
        - hover it and click x -> remove it
     B-roll always covers whole scenes; to cover only part of one,
     split that scene first (S). Every drag can be undone (Ctrl+Z).
     Or in the Inspector: "Cover with B-roll" on the selected scene.
     ("This scene is... B-roll footage" at the bottom takes a scene OUT
     of the video to use its picture as B-roll - it asks first.)

     Or type a command in the box and press Enter,
     or click the quick buttons. Examples:

        cut                  remove this scene
        keep                 bring a cut scene back
        trim start 0.5       remove 0.5 seconds from the beginning
        trim end 1           remove 1 second from the end
        split at 2.5         split the scene at 2.5 seconds
        speed 1.3            make it faster  (slow mo = slower)
        mute                 remove sound from this scene
        zoom in              punch-in zoom
        text: Your title     show a title on screen
        captions off         hide captions for this scene
        remove silences      cut out pauses (jump cuts)

     Commands for the WHOLE video (💬 box, no scene named):
        format 9:16          vertical video for Reels / Shorts
        captions upper       CAPITAL LETTER captions
        captions hinglish    Hindi captions in English letters (aap kaise ho)
        captions hindi       Hindi captions in Hindi script (आप कैसे हो)
        spell daval as double   fix one wrong caption word everywhere
                                (type the word as you SEE it on the page)
        highlight #22C55E    change caption highlight colour
        layout split         cards on top, you below (Brand Kit look)
        headline: bajaj *IPO* aaj   card for scenes without their own
        music on / music off quiet background music under your voice
                             (your own track: put it in Brand Kit\music)
        face up / face down  move yourself up/down in the frame

     Commands for ONE scene (split layout, top panel):
        card: title bajaj *IPO* listing aaj    (*word* = orange)
        card: number 2x+ | listing gain
        card: chart 10,12,11,15 | Nifty 5 days
        card off                               empty top panel
        remove silences      cut pauses everywhere

     B-roll commands:
        broll Stock Market Sensex.mp4   cover this scene with that clip
        broll top / bottom / full       where it shows (top / bottom / full)
        broll from 3                    start 3 s into the clip
        broll off                       remove it from this scene
        role broll / role speaker       what this scene IS
        broll auto   (💬 box, no scene)  place unused B-roll clips

     STORYBOARD (when Claude has planned the edit): click the orange
     "Storyboard" button. Each beat shows the planned frame, cuts,
     zooms and cards. Tick/untick items, click Approve or Reject, write
     notes. Then click "✓ Apply to video & back to Scenes": the
     approved beats are put into your edit right away (no Claude needed,
     one Undo step) and you are back on the Scenes page, where the
     preview updates by itself. "Approve all pending" offers the same.
     Rejected beats with notes go to Claude to rework.
     "← Scenes" goes back without changing anything.

     Made a mistake?  Click  "Undo".
     Want to see the result?  Click  "▶ Your edited video".
     Happy with it?  Click  "Export final".

  TAB 5  EXPORTS
     Watch your finished videos. They are saved in the  Exports  folder.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. ASKING CLAUDE (AI) TO EDIT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Anything that is not in the list above - for example
  "make it 30 seconds" or "✨ Claude: plan the edit" (Scenes) -
  goes to the orange "Claude inbox" at the top right.

  These wait until someone with Claude Code (in VS Code) types:
        process studio inbox
  Claude then does the edit and the answer appears on the page.
  Without Claude Code, everything else still works - only the inbox waits.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. WHERE ARE MY FILES?
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Raw Footage\   your uploaded videos
  References\    your sample/reference files
  Storyboards\   your projects (the edits you made)
  Exports\       finished videos
  Brand Kit\     put your logo here (PNG) to use it in videos
  Library\       stickers, sound effects, previews (+ your own)

  Your videos are NOT uploaded anywhere. Everything stays on this PC.
  To continue a project on another PC, copy these folders for that
  project:  Raw Footage\<project>, References\<project>,
  Storyboards\<project>.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Page doesn't open        -> is the black window still open?
                              If not, double-click Start Studio.bat again.
  "failed" shown in red    -> read the "Activity" box in tab 1;
                              send a screenshot to whoever set this up.
  Slow or stuck rendering  -> close other heavy programs (Chrome tabs,
                              games). One video job runs at a time.
  Setup stopped halfway    -> check the internet and run Setup.bat again.
                              It continues where it stopped.

  Needs: Windows 10 or 11. An NVIDIA graphics card makes it faster
  (not required).
