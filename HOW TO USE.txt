=====================================================================
  CLAUDE VIDEO STUDIO - HOW TO USE  (no technical knowledge needed)
=====================================================================

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. START THE STUDIO
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Double-click:   Start Studio.bat

  - A black window opens and the studio opens in your web browser.
    (If the browser doesn't open, type this in the address bar:
     http://127.0.0.1:8070 )

  - KEEP THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN while you work. It IS the studio.
    You can minimise it. Closing it stops the studio.

  FIRST TIME ON A NEW COMPUTER:
  - The black window asks "Run Setup now? [Y,N]". Press  Y .
  - Setup downloads everything it needs (about 2.5 GB, 10-15 minutes).
    This happens only once. Next time the studio opens right away.
  - You can also run setup yourself: double-click  Setup.bat

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. EDIT A VIDEO (4 tabs at the top of the page)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  "+ New project"  -> give it a name.

  TAB 1  FOOTAGE
     Drag your video(s) into the box, then click  "Analyze footage".
     Wait for "Analysis complete" (about 1 minute per minute of video).
     The video is split into short scenes with the spoken words.

  TAB 2  REFERENCES & BRIEF  (optional)
     Drag in sample videos/images whose style you like.
     Write what you want (e.g. "60-second Instagram reel, fast cuts").
     Choose format (9:16 for Reels/Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube),
     caption colours, logo. Click "Save brief & settings".
     "Caption script": Hindi (हिंदी) or Hinglish (English letters).
     For Hindi videos also set "Language" to Hindi before analyzing.
     "Apply Brand Kit" = your approved look (Brand Kit\brand-kit.md).
     New projects start with it automatically.

  TAB 3  SCENES  (the main editor)
     Click a scene to play it. Type a command in the box and press Enter,
     or click the quick buttons. Examples:

        cut                  remove this scene
        keep                 bring a cut scene back
        trim start 0.5       remove 0.5 seconds from the beginning
        trim end 1           remove 1 second from the end
        split at 2.5         split the scene at 2.5 seconds
        speed 1.3            make it faster  (slow mo = slower)
        mute                 remove sound from this scene
        zoom in              punch-in zoom
        text: Your title     show a title on screen
        captions off         hide captions for this scene
        remove silences      cut out pauses (jump cuts)

     Commands for the WHOLE video (top box):
        format 9:16          vertical video for Reels / Shorts
        captions upper       CAPITAL LETTER captions
        captions hinglish    Hindi captions in English letters (aap kaise ho)
        captions hindi       Hindi captions in Hindi script (आप कैसे हो)
        spell daval as double   fix one wrong caption word everywhere
                                (type the word as you SEE it on the page)
        highlight #22C55E    change caption highlight colour
        layout split         cards on top, you below (Brand Kit look)
        headline: bajaj *IPO* aaj   card for scenes without their own
        music on / music off quiet background music under your voice
                             (your own track: put it in Brand Kit\music)
        face up / face down  move yourself up/down in the frame

     Commands for ONE scene (split layout, top panel):
        card: title bajaj *IPO* listing aaj    (*word* = orange)
        card: number 2x+ | listing gain
        card: chart 10,12,11,15 | Nifty 5 days
        card off                               empty top panel
        remove silences      cut pauses everywhere

     Made a mistake?  Click  "Undo".
     Want to see the result?  Click  "Render preview".
     Happy with it?  Click  "Export final".

  TAB 4  EXPORTS
     Watch your finished videos. They are saved in the  Exports  folder.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. ASKING CLAUDE (AI) TO EDIT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Anything that is not in the list above - for example
  "make it 30 seconds" or "Ask Claude for the first cut" -
  goes to the orange "Claude inbox" at the top right.

  These wait until someone with Claude Code (in VS Code) types:
        process studio inbox
  Claude then does the edit and the answer appears on the page.
  Without Claude Code, everything else still works - only the inbox waits.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. WHERE ARE MY FILES?
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Raw Footage\   your uploaded videos
  References\    your sample/reference files
  Storyboards\   your projects (the edits you made)
  Exports\       finished videos
  Brand Kit\     put your logo here (PNG) to use it in videos

  Your videos are NOT uploaded anywhere. Everything stays on this PC.
  To continue a project on another PC, copy these folders for that
  project:  Raw Footage\<project>, References\<project>,
  Storyboards\<project>.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Page doesn't open        -> is the black window still open?
                              If not, double-click Start Studio.bat again.
  "failed" shown in red    -> read the "Activity" box in tab 1;
                              send a screenshot to whoever set this up.
  Slow or stuck rendering  -> close other heavy programs (Chrome tabs,
                              games). One video job runs at a time.
  Setup stopped halfway    -> check the internet and run Setup.bat again.
                              It continues where it stopped.

  Needs: Windows 10 or 11. An NVIDIA graphics card makes it faster
  (not required).
